# CONTEXT.md — Read Me First (for every chat)

> **The Withering** — first-person dark fantasy open-world survival RPG in **Godot 4.4**.
> Skill-based melee, no spellcasting (magic = items/materials only), gear that grows with you,
> enemies wither to dust. Art: gritty-realism low-poly, slate/indigo base + ember/frost accents.
> Everything is placeholder primitives until step 11. Last updated: 2026-07-14.

## ✅ COMPLETED SYSTEMS (what already works)

**Player core — `Player.gd` (2500 lines: controller + combat + HUD + all menus)**
- **THE LENS FIGHTS WITH YOU** (`cam_anim`, a node between `cam_arm` and the camera, driven by `_update_action_camera` — bob/shake keep their own channel on `camera` untouched): sword swings CARRY the camera ACROSS each cut — a coil against the chamber, then real travel from one side of the frame to the other with the edge (not a symmetric bump) — and the chain RAMPS: swing 1 leans ×1.0, swing 2 ×1.45, the overhead finisher ×1.9 with the deepest dip, then around again (saddle sweeps travel by `mounted_side`), axe/pick chops drive it down into the bite, the bow draw settles it onto the cheek (`bow_draw`-scaled cant + cheek-weld offset), blocking braces it back, the Committed Step shoves it forward, and every LANDED hit fires `cam_punch` — a fast-decaying forward kick. All leans mirror with the Main Hand setting. **In third person the same numbers lean the whole visible BODY** (`body_rig.rotation` — mirror-safe: rotation and scale are separate channels), so a watcher sees the torso commit into cuts and stoop to the pack.
- **THE OLD RUCKSACK** (`pack_rig` on the back, always worn; starting-kit item "Old Rucksack", undroppable — "your whole life is in that rucksack"): worn leather + canvas, top-rolled flap, front pocket with a sewn patch, side pouch, shoulder straps with brass buckles, a battered tin cup dangling off the corner — and a LASHING SPOT on top where the **bedroll bundle** shows whenever a Bedroll is in the pack (`bedroll_bundle.visible`, checked in frame upkeep). **The bedroll stacks to ONE** (`_give_item_dict` refuses a second — "one bed is all the rucksack will lash on"; `_pack_bedroll` only folds the world-bed if the pack actually took it). **Opening the inventory is an ANIMATION** (`pack_reach`): the eyes drop to the satchel (cam_anim pitch+roll), the FP hands leave the frame (`hands_root.position` dips), and in TP the right arm swings back to the rucksack's flap — closing the menu brings it all home.
- FP movement with momentum: WASD, sprint, jump, auto step-up, Ctrl dash (stamina + i-frames), **Space mantle-climb** (`_try_climb`/`_update_climb`: grabbable ledge ≤2.65 m beats jumping — rise-then-haul animation, hands plant, camera dips; works mid-air; 8 stamina; cancelled by knockdown). **Bump absorber** (`_eye_smooth` in `_apply_step_smooth`): small grounded body hops on lumpy voxel rock reach the eye as an eased glide — feet bump, view floats. **Anti-wall-peek**: camera.near = 0.02 + a full-width HEAD SPHERE collider (r 0.34 at y 1.56 — the capsule cap narrows up top and let the lens be walked into overhangs/thin walls). **Camera modes (V)**: head→`cam_arm`→camera; `_apply_armor_visuals`), the right arm always present (FP still hides it while the sword-hand viewmodel performs), and a **body-held TORCH** (`tp_torch` on the left fist, arm raised to carry it): same flame + flickering OmniLight as the viewmodel brand, so TP nights and caves are actually LIT (the hidden FP viewmodel used to take the light with it). **TP is fully PLAYABLE now**: body-held twins of every weapon (`tp_sword`/`tp_shield`/`tp_bow`/`tp_pick`/`tp_axe`, `_update_tp_gear`; sword rebuilt with the equipped metal) and the arms ACT OUT combat from the same state machines the viewmodels use — three-beat sword cuts per combo step + saddle sweeps (`_tp_swing_arm_pose` on Enemy's shared `_cut_arc`), pick/axe hoist-and-drive chops (`_tp_chop_arm_pose`), shield raised across the body while blocking, bow arm out + string hand pinned while drawing — impact frames land exactly when damage does. **Aim/interact ranges measure from the body's EYE, not the camera** (`_aim_origin()`: pickaxe vein/dig ray, look+E targets, arrow spawn, Q-drop toss) — in TP the camera hangs 2.6 m back, which used to put everything "out of reach" and made TP feel dead. **Stances (C cycles stand→crouch→PRONE)**: crouch ×0.45 speed / eye 1.08; prone ×0.22 speed / eye 0.45 + head pushed forward, slow heavy ease + settling roll pulse (`_stance_settle_pulse`) = the going-prone animation; tall-grass aggro ×0.6/×0.35/×0.22 by stance; jump/climb stands you up; collision unchanged (TODO crawl-under-gaps). **Out-of-bounds rescue**: the World safety net also teleports home if ever beyond ±103.5 (stuck-outside-border insurance).
- Visible first-person body (torso/legs/hands), swinging-arm walk cycle, Alt sheathe/unsheathe to hip scabbard + draw-slash. **You spawn SHEATHED** (`_ready`) — steel comes out when something earns it. **Combat — in `Player.gd`, telegraphs in `Enemy.gd`**
- LMB 1-2-3 combo (finisher bonus, ~0.75s reset) with realistic 3-phase cuts (chamber → whip → follow-through); damage lands at visual impact.
- **THE COMMITTED STEP** (`_commit_swing`/`_enemy_ahead`, `committed`): Ctrl DURING a cut — or a cut started mid-dash — spends the dash on the blade instead of your footwork. The step drives forward along the swing line (the dash direction is overridden to body-forward), the blow lands at **×2.05** with **+0.9 m reach**, and it costs **30 stamina on top of the swing**, paid up front whether or not it connects ("Committed to nothing" if it doesn't). Guarded so it never eats your defence: it only fires with an enemy in the forward cone, and an unaffordable commit falls through to an ordinary dodge-dash. Landing one feeds **The Committed Step** (dex); landing one at ≤30% health also feeds **Nothing Left to Lose** (con).
- RMB block: shield = full negate, bare sword = half. Enemy strong attacks guard-break (2s block disable + knockback + camera shake).
- **Parry**: block raised ≤0.18s before impact = no damage/stamina, attacker staggers, beats guard-breakers (`PARRY_WINDOW`).
- No ghost hits: attacks both ways need line-of-sight + shared height band.

**Weapons (1/2/3/4 select)**
- **1 Sword** — the combo loop above; material-driven (see below).
- **2 Bow** — hold-to-draw (damage/speed ride the draw, DEX quickens, STR powers), real arrow projectiles with drop (`Arrow.gd`), missed arrows stick in terrain and are retrievable, 20-arrow ammo in inventory.
- **3 Iron Pickaxe** — chop with impact-timed bite; mines ore veins AND digs cave rock (Caves 2.0). Swords can't mine. **Carried upright like the sword** (`PICK_REST_*` vertical; swings blend from the carry into arcs authored on `PICK_BASE_*` over the first fifth — same trick as the sword). The axe does the same (`AXE_REST_*`/`AXE_BASE_*`).
- **4 War Axe** — heavy one-handed cleaver (`_try_axe_swing`/`_update_axe`/`_do_axe_hit`): ×1.35 base damage, slower, no combo ladder — two ALTERNATING authored **SIDE swings** (forehand hauled over the right shoulder and swept flat right-to-left through the trunk line, then the backhand answer — a feller's strokes, and the camera rolls with each sweep), impact-timed damage, hits everything in the arc, LOS-checked. No material matchups yet (TODO: fold into metal system at step 4/5). Can't block with it; offhand shield/torch stay up. **CHOPS TREES — `ChopTree.gd` owns all of it now** (`Player._chop_tree` just throws chips and delegates to `chop_hit`): a tree is a PROCEDURAL trunk (rings of faceted geometry, vertex-coloured bark) and every bite **removes a V-shaped wedge** from the struck side (`_radius_at`: deepest at the notch mid-height, closing to nothing top and bottom, wrapping a bounded arc — a triangular bite, not an added cube), pale heartwood exposed inside it, deepening past the trunk's centre by the last swing. Then the trunk **breaks on that notch** (`fell`): a stump stays rooted wearing the wedge (group `"tree_stumps"`, saved), the `_upper` piece hinges over away from the axe (1.5 s eased tip). On impact (`_crash`) — **the canopy comes apart**: every leaf breaks off as its own `FallingLitter` and planes down, MOST dropping out of the crown so the litter prints the SHAPE OF THE FALLEN CROWN on the ground — sampled over `LEAF_SPREAD` ×1.7 the cone's radius with an outward bias (a canopy is mostly rim) and an outward push scaled by where each leaf sat, so the print INFLATES rather than smears — and ~22% catching one shared gust bearing and travelling 3-15 m; and **the trunk splits into `round(height)` logs** (2-8, `CarryLog.gd`) that kick off the fall line, tumble, and settle. Plus chips + sticks + a distance-scaled shake (`Player.tree_crash_shake`). Trees no longer hand you Wood — they hand you logs.
- **Fall damage** (`_apply_fall_damage`, fires on touchdown in `_update_gait`): safe to ~6 m (11 m/s), then 6.5 dmg per m/s over; ≥17.5 m/s also folds you into the knockdown; lethal falls kill. Bypasses block/i-frames; mantling mid-air zeroes fall speed (the grab saves you).

**Enemies — `Enemy.gd` base (819 lines) + per-mob subclasses**
- **NERVE IS A STAT — creatures BREAK** (`nerve`/`rout_speed`/`_break_and_run`/`_do_rout`): hurt one past its `nerve` fraction of max health and the fight simply stops. It abandons the windup, drops its grudge, refuses to climb, and RUNS — on a stumbling limp at `chase_speed × rout_speed` (kobold 2.9 m/s vs its 4.6 hunting pace; player sprint ≈ 8), so running it down is a choice, not a race. It calms once it's 1.5× leash away or after 20 s. Per-mob: kobold breaks at 45%, goblin 32%, boar 30%, orc 15%, ogre 12% — **skeleton and dark knight never** (`nerve = 0`: bone has no nerve, and whatever is in that plate doesn't retreat). Horses keep `nerve = 0` because their own panic/bolt system already owns flight. One log line per break, in the creature's own words.
- Shared AI: calm-wander → agitated, circle/orbit then dart in, telegraphed glowing windups, flinch, caught-mid-attack retreat, white-flash triangle-shatter wither death, far-off mobs sleep (CPU). **ALL CREATURES CLIMB** (`_update_climb`, per-mob `climb_speed` — kobold 3.4 … ogre 2.1 … horse 2.3): climbing is a REAL part of the hunt, not a stuck-recovery. Three ways onto a wall: **READ it** (target above → a staggered forward ray finds steep rock and the mob commits like it was always the plan, no shoving), **LATCH it** (leapt/fell against the face mid-air), or **SHOVE it** (plain blocked past a per-mob patience roll). On the face it claws upward via the shared gait AND **drifts along the wall toward the target's bearing** (an angled hunter's route, not an elevator), then hauls over the lip with a landing surge — mantling onto a boulder no longer makes you safe. Any hit cuts the grip (shoot the climber off the wall); calm = back to earth; **HORSES CLIMB TOO NOW** — theirs is a FLIGHT climb (`climb_away = true`): the trigger is a close threat rather than prey overhead, the drift angles AWAY from the hunter across the face, and being above the threat is the goal, not a reason to let go. **INFIGHTING** (`foe`/`_target()`): melee swings splash ×0.6 damage onto any OTHER creature in the arc; a creature struck by a creature sets a MUTUAL grudge (`take_damage` attacker hook — signature extended with default args so player-shaped calls hit mobs too) — both retarget each other until one dies/leashes out; horses hit by creatures just flee (no trust break, no kick — that's player-only).
- Roster (ascending): **Kobold** (lunge/tail-spin) < **Goblin** (pounce/flurry) < **Skeleton** (smash/sweep) < **Orc** (charge/thrust/slash) / **Ogre** (bite/throw/charge) < **DarkKnight** (shield-bash/dark-combo), plus neutral **Boar** (big-arc circling charge). Two specials per humanoid; per-mob throw power (boar 5 → ogre 8.5); blocking = never thrown.
- Boars roam the overworld; everything else spawns in cave packs (kobold 6-10, goblin 3-6, skeleton 4-7, orcs/ogres deeper, champion room = dark knight leading orcs).
- Central kill accounting: `Enemy._die → Player.on_mob_slain` (so bow/pickaxe kills count everywhere).

**Loot & pickups — `Pickup.gd`**
- Death bursts tier-colored XP orbs (green→purple, 2/5/8/11 XP) + gold coins; they scatter, fall, rest, magnet to you on walk-over. Loot rules: beasts = XP only; undead = no coins.
- **EVERYTHING FALLS HONESTLY**: bodies are never ground — orbs, coins, dropped items, and debris that come down on a player or creature GLANCE OFF sideways and keep falling (`_glance_off` in Pickup/DroppedItem; RockDebris deflects in both its fall and roll). And rest is never final: resting loot re-checks its footing on a staggered ~0.5 s clock (`_refoot`/`_recheck_footing`) — dig the floor out from under it (or let a sleep-shift redraw the cave) and it drops again, all the way to the true ground.

**Horses & riding — `Horse.gd` + `SaddledHorse.gd` (step 6 first pass)**
- Two kinds, one bestiary page: **wild** herds (2×3-4, far tree line) graze/flee — skittish radius 8m, never rideable; **saddled** (2 near spawn) are calm and mountable with **F**. **HEARTS**: horses take 3 HITS (any hit = 1 heart, `Horse.hearts`/`_take_heart_hit`), regrow +1 per 3 unharmed seconds. **WHO sees the tally depends on who should care**: ridden = the `♥♥♡` pips live on the RIDER'S HUD (`Player.mount_hearts`, above the bars — flares when an enemy catches your mount, idles dim at full hearts); loose horse hurt by the WORLD = billboard pips over its head; **your own strikes get no display** (the trust break says the rest). Creature attackers cause pure flight (no trust break/kick — player-only consequences; a RIDDEN horse hit by a creature stays under your reins). **THE MOUNT IS THE GUARD** (`Player.take_damage` → `Horse.rider_shielded_hit`): a hit that lands on a MOUNTED rider does ZERO player damage — it costs the horse a heart instead (HUD flash; no trust loss). Spend the last heart under a rider (shielded hits OR creature splash) and the horse doesn't die: **`_enough_buck`** — rears, throws you (`thrown_from_mount` knockdown), and bolts alive; hearts regrow and it will carry you again. Only unridden horses (or your own blade) can beat one to death. **HORSES SHUN CAVES BEFORE THEY EVER REACH ONE** (`CAVE_SHUN_R` 16 m / `CAVE_SHUN_HARD` 8 m, `_shun_caves`/`_cave_repulse`, mouths read from the `"cave_regions"` group and re-asked every 3-6 s so a mouth torn open under a grazing herd is noticed): a loose horse grazing near a mouth simply drifts away — an unbothered walk at 0.9-1.5× wander speed that happens to always point elsewhere, still swaying on its own bearing so it reads as wandering off rather than being pushed. Flight consults the same repulsion, so a spooked horse bends its line AROUND a mouth instead of bolting down one. **THE SCRAMBLE — a RIDDEN horse takes rock faces** (`_try_scramble`/`_update_scramble` in `_do_ridden`): press the reins INTO a steep wall (≥0.55 head-on, face steeper than 0.45 normal-y, within ~1.7 m) and it rears onto it and claws up at `climb_speed` 2.3 — the reins steer the drift along the face, slack reins detach, the top-out is a shoulders-first haul onto the ledge (rider and all), and a rear-up that never finds stone within 1.2 s gives up quietly (hard approach press until hooves touch, then a steady one — `_scramble_touched`). Cave panic still outranks it: no scrambling out of the forbidden dark. **CAVES ARE REFUSED** (`CAVE_PANIC_Y −2.2`, `_cave_panic`): ride into a cave throat and it rears, bucks you, and BOLTS. **The panic bolt is DISTANCE-honest** (`_start_panic`/`_do_panic_flee`/`_panic_cleared`): underground it gallops for its last daylight footing (`_surface_pos`), stamps the doorstep where hooves find open ground (`_panic_from`), then runs until it's **8-13 m** (rolled per panic) past that before settling to graze (14 s safety clock backs it). A panicking or zero-heart horse refuses the saddle (`can_carry`, checked in `_try_mount_toggle`). Terror, not betrayal — trust survives it.
- Riding: player capsule off, rides `saddle_world()`; camera-relative WASD reins (horse turns like an animal, 2.6 rad/s), Shift gallop 11.0 (player sprint ≈ 8), Space jump. Sword only; LMB = flat saddle sweeps via `MOUNTED_KEYS` — look >0.35 rad left/right picks the side, ahead alternates. No blocking/dash/finishers mounted.
- **Kick + trust**: any player damage to any horse sets `trust_broken` forever (it flees you, never carries you). Within 3.6m it retaliates — wheels rump-first and kicks (14 dmg, `Player.horse_kick`): parry or dash i-frames counter it, anything else = **knockdown** (`kd_phase` fall→down→rise, ~2.2s, camera drops+rolls, zero protection, inputs dead, enemies keep attacking; **ragdoll landing**: the head BOUNCES off the dirt with a damping wobble (`kd_bounce`) and a ground ray under the head clamps the lens ABOVE the surface every phase — no more sinking under lumpy rock and getting spat back out). Your own swings can never hit your mount (excluded in `_do_melee_hit`); the buck-off (`thrown_from_mount`) still fires if a ridden horse is hurt some other way or dies under you. Central to it: `Player._start_knockdown` is reusable for future knockdown sources.

## 🔁 HOW IT FITS TOGETHER (the playable loop)
Explore the forest → find a cave mouth → fight telegraphed pack combat (combo/block/parry/dodge, bow for pulls) → kills burst XP orbs + coins (`Pickup.gd` → `Stats.gd`) → level stats + progression trees pay out for *how* you play → bestiary entries + matchups unlock by doing → mine silver/meteoric veins with the pickaxe → first ore forges a new material sword → swap metals per enemy family (silver in the crypt-cave, steel vs the humanoid pack) → push deeper rooms → dark knight champion. Dev shortcuts while building: M spawns mobs, Armory grabs any sword.

Three progression layers by design: **you** (stats/levels) · **your style** (weapon loops — being built) · **your specific sword** (evolution/durability — next up). Difficulty philosophy: hard but fair; power should change *how* you fight, not just numbers.
