# Name Candidates (Valheim-energy) — the big list

> Working title: **The Withering**. Wanted: short, mythic, ownable — reads like a
> place or a doom. Before committing: search the name on Steam + a trademark check.

## Norse/heim-style (closest to Valheim)
- **Witherheim** — the most literal "our Valheim"; home of the withering
- **Myrkheim** — real Norse root (*myrk* = dark); the dark home
- **Duskheim** — the signature 17:30 dusk, made into a realm
- **Ashgard** — Asgard bent toward ash and ruin
- **Dustgard** — the stronghold of dust
- **Niflmark** — mist-borderland (*Nifl* as in Niflheim)
- **Myrkholt** — "dark wood"; fits the forest overworld
- **Askholt** — ash-wood (Norse *aska*)
- **Grimmark** — grim borderland
- **Hulderheim** — from *huldra/hidden folk*; the hidden home
- **Jordheim** — earth-home; a world that keeps opening underfoot
- **Steinmark** — stone-march; mining country
- **Ulfheim** — wolf-home; feral wilderness energy
- **Vardheim** — *varða* = cairn/warden; the warded home
- **Skuggmark** — *skuggi* = shadow; shadow-march

## One word, heavy
- **Wither** — blunt, ownable, matches the death effect
- **Duskfall** — day/night is a core system; nightfall as a threat
- **Hollowing** — the caves, the withered, the world emptying
- **Gloaming** — the old word for dusk; moody and real
- **Emberdeep** — torchlight + meteoric glow in the deeps
- **Undermere** — the black still lake down there
- **Duststone** — mining + what enemies leave behind
- **Sundering** — the shifting caves; the earth tearing open
- **Delve** — short, verby, entirely about going down
- **Underdelve** — the same, made a place
- **Gloamrot** — dusk + decay; nasty in a good way
- **Huskfall** — the withered as husks, falling to dust
- **Stonewake** — the earth waking under you (quakes, shifts)
- **Ashenfall** — softer sibling of Ashfall (which is taken)
- **Mirefall** — bogs and dark water
- **Dimshire** — homely word gone wrong
- **Palewood** — the forest, drained
- **Wraithwood** — what walks in it at night
